How it works?

End-to-end intelligent classification pipeline from camera capture to immediate environmental guidance.

01

Input Acquisition

Capture a live photo using your phone/webcam or upload an existing image. The input is normalized with aspect-ratio preservation to prevent geometric distortion.

02

MobileNetV2 Deep Learning

A lightweight, inverted-residual convolutional neural network extracts spatial patterns, textures, and material contours to compute softmax class probabilities.

03

Segregation & Eco Guidance

The system returns the predicted category, confidence level, designated municipal bin color, recyclability instructions, and environmental impact facts.
